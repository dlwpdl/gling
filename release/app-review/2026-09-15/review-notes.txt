Reporting, blocking and account deletion are reachable as described below with the review account.

1. Review access
Tap the profile icon at the top-right of the feed, then "심사용 계정 로그인 / Review access". Enter the App Review Information username/password and tap "심사 계정으로 로그인 / Sign in". This account has ordinary member permissions, no admin privileges. No Kakao/Apple/Google account, OTP, payment or presence in Canada is required.

2. Main flows
Browse the feed without login; choose Vancouver, Toronto or Edmonton manually. Tabs: Today, Meetups, Write, Chat, Me. Signup uses native Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In or Kakao, then nickname/city and terms/privacy/external-AI consent. No public email signup.
Write offers a story post (free: 1/day) or a listing post for housing, used items or vehicles (up to 5 open, 14-day life, bump once per 24h). Post details: comments with one nested reply level, edit/delete own comments, like, save, report, block. Tap an author for a mini profile with a chat request. Direct chats need recipient acceptance; meetups need host approval. Free: 3 meetup slots, 3 direct-chat slots; a closed direct chat locks the requester's slot 24h. Delete account: Me > Settings > Delete account (Apple-linked accounts reauthorize first). Seeded posts are initial review content, not real customer activity.

3. Subscriptions (submitted with this version)
Me > Membership: Gling Plus and Premium, monthly/yearly, via StoreKit/RevenueCat. The current launch price is shown with the planned regular price struck through, labeled 출시 기념가 (launch price). Tier is never shown to other members; it only raises the member's own limits. No credit packs or paid promotion.

4. External services
Supabase (Auth, PostgreSQL, Storage, Realtime, Edge Functions). Apple, Google (native SDK), Kakao sign-in. OpenAI API: optional photo-to-draft help and server-side safety analysis of all new posts/comments/messages after consent; humans decide bans; admin access audited; chats not end-to-end encrypted. RevenueCat/StoreKit. Google AdMob: a native ad card after every 7 feed posts. GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, Resend (operator alerts), Expo/React Native.

5. Regions
Same features and rules everywhere; city selection controls content, not device location. No GPS permission. Interface in Korean.

6. Regulated services/material
A public community, not a medical, financial, gambling, escrow or rent-payment provider; listings are classifieds and the app processes no payments between members. A seeded Vancouver photo credits Vlad D/Unsplash (https://unsplash.com/photos/19aJ-K6fUmY; license https://unsplash.com/license).
