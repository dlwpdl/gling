Gling 1.0.1 (30) is a Korean-language public community for Vancouver, Toronto and Edmonton, Canada. It is open to the public, not restricted to an organization.

CHANGES FOR RE-REVIEW
Before signup or login, the screen now requires two separate, initially unchecked agreements:
- [필수] 이용약관과 커뮤니티 행동 기준에 동의합니다. (Terms of Use and community rules)
- [필수] 개인정보 수집·이용에 동의합니다. (Personal-data collection and use)
Each agreement has a visible URL and an independently clickable full-policy link. Neither is preselected. Apple, Google, Kakao and Review access cannot continue until both are checked. The account-bound agreement version and server timestamp are recorded after authentication. The notice explicitly prohibits objectionable content, harassment, hate and threats and explains content removal and account restrictions.

Optional name/date-of-birth collection remains optional. Separate explicit OpenAI safety-processing consent is presented during profile onboarding.
Build 30 also adds an optional free cover photo for one-off and recurring meetups, and group-chat members and message timestamps.

REVIEW ACCESS
Open Me (나) or the profile icon, then 심사용 계정 로그인 / Review access. Check both required agreements, enter the existing App Review Information username/password, and tap 심사 계정으로 로그인 / Sign in. This account has ordinary member permissions. No social account, OTP, purchase or presence in Canada is required. The same agreements apply to this review route.

SAFETY AND MAIN FLOWS
Guests can browse public posts and choose a city manually. Post detail offers reporting and user blocking. Direct chats require recipient acceptance; meetups require host approval. Automated safety analysis covers all new posts, comments and conversation messages after consent; authorized human review is audited and humans decide permanent bans. Chats are not end-to-end encrypted.
Delete account: Me > Settings > Delete account; Apple-linked accounts reauthenticate. Seeded examples are review content, not real-user activity.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
Gling Plus/Premium monthly and yearly subscriptions are included in the existing review submission. They increase the member's own limits; membership tier is not shown to other members. Me > Membership shows localized prices and store purchase/restore controls. No payment is needed for the review flows above.

EXTERNAL SERVICES AND LOCATION
Supabase, Apple/Google/Kakao authentication, OpenAI, RevenueCat/StoreKit and Google AdMob (in-feed ads). Photo-to-draft assistance is optional. Foreground location sharing is optional: users can instead select a city manually. No continuous/background location tracking. The same features apply in every supported city. Interface is Korean.
