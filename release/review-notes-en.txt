Gling is a Korean-language community app for Vancouver and Toronto, Canada. Other listed cities are not open yet. The public feed can be browsed without signing in. Account features require authentication and onboarding.

Review access (build 5 or later): Open the app, tap the person icon ("나", Profile) at the top-right, next to Search, then "심사용 계정 로그인 / Review access". Enter the supplied demo username and password and tap "심사 계정으로 로그인 / Sign in". This pre-created account has normal member permissions and the same app functionality. No personal Kakao or Apple account, OTP, phone verification, payment, or physical presence in Canada is needed. Public email registration is not available.

Choose Vancouver or Toronto manually. Review post details, posting, comments, conversations, reporting and blocking. Demo posts and authors are seeded content for initial review, not evidence of real user activity. Normal member posting and anti-abuse limits also apply to this account.

Account deletion is under Profile > Settings > Delete account. Apple-linked accounts reauthorize before provider token revocation and deletion. An external account-deletion request page is also available at https://gling.ej-entertainment.com/account-deletion.

Ordinary members use Kakao sign-in, with native Sign in with Apple also available on iOS. Users must accept the terms, privacy policy and external AI safety-processing disclosure before creating or continuing a profile.

All new posts, comments and private/group messages undergo server-side safety analysis, and authorized admin access is audited. Conversations are not end-to-end encrypted. AI does not make final permanent-ban decisions. Reporting, blocking and content filtering are available.

Camera and photo-library access are optional and requested when selecting or taking a photo. City selection does not use device GPS. This version contains no advertising or in-app purchases.
