CHANGES FOR RE-REVIEW — SIGNUP CONSENT
After social authentication, a new member sees the nickname/city profile-setup screen BEFORE profile creation or member-only access. This screen has initially unchecked, separate required agreements for Terms/community rules, personal-data collection/use, and external AI (OpenAI) safety processing. Each has a View link or expandable explanation. A select-all control and individual checkboxes are available. The required agreements must be accepted to finish signup; their versions and server timestamps are recorded. Optional name/date-of-birth collection has its own optional agreement and is not required for signup or meetup applications. Consent is collected on this profile step, not before the social-login button.

SAFETY AND FEATURES
Guests can browse and select a city manually. Post detail offers reporting and blocking. Direct chats require recipient acceptance; meetups require host approval. Automated safety analysis covers all new posts, comments and conversation messages after consent; authorized human review is audited and humans decide permanent bans. Chats are not end-to-end encrypted.
Build 32 adds optional recommended meetup ages; people outside the range or without a birth date may apply. Birth dates are self-reported, explicitly unverified, and do not raise verification level. New-purpose consent is required before using previously supplied birth dates for age guidance. Optional cover photos and group-chat members/timestamps are also included.
Delete account: Me > Settings > Delete account; Apple-linked accounts reauthenticate. Seeded examples are review content, not real-user activity.
