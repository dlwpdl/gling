# Child safety response — prepared 2026-09-21

Status: owner confirmed on 2026-09-21 that gling@ej-entertainment.com is the contact to use, in response to the request for the person responsible for inbox monitoring and authority reporting. The app owner is the responsible contact. This procedure is the operational response guide; it does not assert that any actual incident has occurred or been reported.

1. Assigned owner monitors the designated child-safety inbox and existing admin safety/report queues. Record the owner and backup outside the public policy; verify inbox access without sending unsolicited test messages.
2. Treat credible immediate danger as urgent. Contact local emergency services when warranted (911 in Canada). Do not wait for an AI decision or for conclusive evidence when an applicable reporting duty already arises.
3. In existing audited admin tools, identify content/user IDs and timestamps; restrict access to suspected harmful material. Do not ask users to download, forward or attach suspected illegal imagery. Do not copy it into email, AI tools, source control, ordinary screenshots or support tickets.
4. Separate public removal from evidence destruction. Before irreversible deletion or routine account deletion affects evidence, coordinate lawful preservation and restricted access with the responsible authority. Preserve only what is required and already lawfully controlled. Follow the authority's secure transfer instructions; record the case reference, recipient, legal basis, timestamp and retention deadline in restricted operational records.
5. For Canada, distinguish reporting a public URL/IP to Cybertip.ca under section 2 from notifying police under section 3 where there are reasonable grounds that this service is or was used for an offence. Cybertip's provider form expressly distinguishes these cases. Check the current legislation and applicable provincial duties when handling an incident; do not treat a general Cybertip report as automatically discharging every obligation. The currently in-force section 4 specifies 21-day preservation following section 3 notice, subject to other lawful orders; the legislation also lists not-yet-in-force amendments separately. Confirm the applicable rule at incident time.
6. Human review determines account/content actions; audit all admin access. Communicate only necessary outcome information to the reporter without exposing victim identities or investigation details. Document closure and lawful expiry of retained records.
